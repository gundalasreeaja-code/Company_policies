ABC TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.
EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy explains the different types of leave available to employees of ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. and the procedure for requesting leave.

2. Annual Leave

Employees who have completed one year of continuous service are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees who have completed less than one year of service are entitled to annual leave on a pro-rata basis.

3. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.

If an employee takes more than 2 consecutive working days of sick leave, a medical certificate may be requested by the company.

4. Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 6 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Casual leave is intended for short-term personal requirements.

5. Leave Request Procedure

Employees should submit their leave request through the company's employee portal at least 3 working days before the intended leave date.

Emergency leave should be reported to the employee's manager as soon as reasonably possible.

6. Leave Approval

Leave requests must be approved by the employee's reporting manager.

Submitting a leave request does not automatically mean that the leave has been approved.

7. Carry Forward

Employees may carry forward up to 10 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year.

Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward.

8. Public Holidays

Public holidays declared by the company are separate from annual, sick, and casual leave.

9. Policy Review

The company may review and update this policy when required.
