ABC TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.
EMPLOYEE ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy defines the attendance and working-hour requirements for employees.

2. Standard Working Hours

The standard working hours for employees are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to follow the working schedule applicable to their department.

3. Attendance Recording

Employees must record their attendance using the company's approved attendance system.

Employees are responsible for ensuring that their attendance records are accurate.

4. Late Arrival

Employees who expect to arrive late should inform their reporting manager as soon as possible.

Frequent late arrival may be reviewed by the employee's manager.

5. Early Departure

Employees who need to leave before the end of their scheduled working hours should obtain approval from their reporting manager.

6. Absence

Employees who are unable to attend work should inform their reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Unreported absence may be treated as unauthorized absence.

7. Remote Employees

Employees working remotely must follow the working hours applicable to their role and must remain available through approved communication channels.

8. Attendance Corrections

If an employee identifies an error in their attendance record, they should submit a correction request through the employee portal.

9. Monitoring

Managers may review attendance records to ensure compliance with company requirements.

10. Policy Review

The company may modify this policy when required.
