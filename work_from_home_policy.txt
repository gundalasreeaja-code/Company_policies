ABC TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.
WORK FROM HOME POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which employees may work from home.

2. Eligibility

Employees who have completed at least 3 months of employment may request work from home.

Work-from-home eligibility may depend on the employee's role and business requirements.

3. Work From Home Days

Eligible employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week.

The employee's manager may approve a different arrangement when business requirements require it.

4. Approval

Employees must obtain approval from their reporting manager before working from home.

A work-from-home request should normally be submitted at least 1 working day in advance.

5. Working Hours

Employees working from home must follow their normal working hours from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Employees are expected to remain available through approved company communication channels during working hours.

6. Attendance

Employees working from home must mark their attendance through the company's attendance system.

Failure to record attendance may be treated as an attendance issue.

7. Company Equipment

Employees may use company-provided laptops and other approved equipment for work.

Company equipment must be handled carefully and used primarily for authorized business activities.

8. Confidential Information

Employees must ensure that confidential company information is not visible or accessible to unauthorized persons while working from home.

9. Policy Exceptions

Any exception to this policy requires approval from the employee's manager and the relevant department.

10. Policy Review

The company may review this policy based on business requirements.
