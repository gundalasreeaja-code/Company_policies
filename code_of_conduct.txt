ABC TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.
EMPLOYEE CODE OF CONDUCT

1. Purpose

The Code of Conduct defines the expected professional behaviour of employees at ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

2. Professional Behaviour

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, vendors, and visitors with professionalism and respect.

Employees should communicate honestly and professionally.

3. Workplace Respect

Employees must maintain a workplace environment that is free from discrimination, harassment, intimidation, and inappropriate behaviour.

4. Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company information, customer information, business records, and intellectual property.

Confidential information must not be shared with unauthorized persons.

5. Company Resources

Company computers, software, networks, and other resources must be used responsibly and primarily for authorized business purposes.

6. Conflicts of Interest

Employees should disclose situations that may create an actual or potential conflict between their personal interests and the interests of the company.

7. Gifts and Business Courtesies

Employees should not accept gifts or benefits that could improperly influence business decisions.

Any gift or business courtesy that may create a conflict should be reported to the employee's manager.

8. Security

Employees must follow company information-security requirements and protect their passwords and access credentials.

Employees must not share their passwords with other people.

9. Reporting Violations

Employees who become aware of a potential violation of this Code of Conduct should report it through the appropriate company reporting channel.

10. Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action in accordance with company procedures.

11. Policy Review

The company may review and update this Code of Conduct when necessary.
